Privacy Policy

App: Smart Calculator & Converter | Last Updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Calculator & Converter ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Your privacy is our top priority. Smart Calculator & Converter is designed as a secure, offline-first calculation and utility tool. All your calculations, custom tape notes, unit conversions, and vault items are processed and stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Use

We do not collect, harvest, or sell any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location. The information processed by the application includes:

  • Calculation & Tool Data: Input numbers, tape notes, and converted units remain strictly within your device's local memory and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Secret Vault Data: Photos, notes, and contacts placed inside the Secret Calculator Vault are stored in the app's sandboxed local private directory. They are never backed up or uploaded to the cloud or third-party servers.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Required exclusively if you choose to use the Secret Vault feature to import, take, or hide private photos. Access is strictly on-device.
  • Contacts: Required exclusively if you use the Secret Contacts feature to store or dial private numbers. We do not export or read your general address book.
  • Internet & Network State: Used solely to serve contextual advertisements via Google AdMob and check network availability.
  • Vibrate: Used to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping keypad buttons.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect anonymous device information solely for analytics, diagnostic, and advertising purposes:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads): Used to display banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements. AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and crash diagnostics to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Policies. Read more at Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take immediate action.

6. Data Security

We implement standard local security measures to protect your data. Your vault content is accessible only through your configured Master PIN or Decoy PIN. We recommend keeping your PIN confidential.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
