# Privacy Policy for Smart Calculator & Converter

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Application:** Smart Calculator & Converter  

Smart Calculator & Converter ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage for our mobile application on Google Play.

---

### 1. Overview
Smart Calculator & Converter is built offline-first. All calculations, history logs, unit conversions, and Secret Calculator Vault files remain strictly on your local device.

### 2. Information We Process
- **Calculation & Smart Tape Data:** Processed locally in real-time. No calculations are sent to external servers.
- **Secret Vault Content:** Secret photos, notes, and contacts are stored in the application's isolated private sandbox directory on your device.

### 3. Permissions Used
- **Camera & Photos/Storage:** Used exclusively to capture or import photos into your private vault upon your explicit request.
- **Contacts:** Used only if you choose to store hidden numbers in the Secret Contacts feature.
- **Internet:** Required solely for ad serving via Google AdMob.
- **Vibrate:** Tactile haptic feedback when pressing keys.

### 4. Third-Party Advertising
The app uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display ads. Google may collect pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) and crash diagnostics to serve relevant ads according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 5. Children's Privacy
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
